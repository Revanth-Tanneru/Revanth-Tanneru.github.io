Decahedron net designer

Generating tessellations for 2-, 3-, 4-, 6- and 8-way symmetries is easier to do than pentagonal tessellations, because you need to cover the plane aperiodically. There aren’t any tools to make a custom-shaped tile that creates an aperiodic pattern with fivefold (72°) rotational symmetry, so I made one.

✋ Drag the orange & blue dots

Tile logic

The shaded tile is one fifth of a pentagon. The orange curve (centre → corner) is copied around the pentagon centre. The blue curve (corner → edge midpoint) is copied again, turned 180° around that midpoint, to finish the edge. Dots: eye (black), optional gill line (green).
