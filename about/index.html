    I began my career as a Software Developer at LoginRadius. There, I built an AI chatbot for customer enquiries, contributed to full-stack web development, and created a CLI tool for the business dashboard.

  3. 03 2021 – 2023

    Following this, I co-founded and served as Lead Designer for Tripping Sapiens — a student apparel brand I designed from the ground up that sold over 2,000 units at cultural festivals. This experience sparked my shift from software development to full-time design. see the project →

  4. 04 2021 – 2022

    Visual & Motion Designer @ Teachmint

    I went on to work as a Visual & Motion Designer at Teachmint, developing cohesive visual identities and animated assets that significantly boosted engagement across their ed-tech platforms. see the project →

  5. 05 2024 – 2025

    MA Graphic Design · Student Ambassador @ Leeds Arts University

    I later pursued my MA in Graphic Design at Leeds Arts University, where I also worked as a Student Ambassador supporting prospective applicants from underrepresented backgrounds.

  6. 06 2024 – 2025

    Research Facilitator @ Leeds Arts University

    Alongside my studies, I served as a Research Facilitator, conducting and analysing focus groups for a three-year project exploring Black, Asian and ethnic minority student experiences in art education.

  7. 07 2025 – now

    Store Enabler @ MathsCity

    My interest in making ideas accessible then led me to the UK’s first maths discovery centre, MathsCity, as a Store Enabler, helping visitors engage with mathematical concepts through tactile and spatial exhibits. see the project →

  8. 08 2025 – now

    Event Lead & Ambassador @ City of Maths

    I now work as an Event Lead & Ambassador for City of Maths, designing and delivering interactive workshops in schools and representing the organisation at outreach events. see the project →

[ what holds it together ]

Creativity should be accessible.

My research and practice are held together by one belief: creativity should be accessible. Not everyone has expensive software or a studio — but everyone has paper, a photocopier, and curiosity. Most of my favourite work is built from exactly that.
