[ about ]

Revanth
Tanneeru

I’m a designer, researcher and science communicator interested in the space where visual art, mathematics and education overlap.

I started as a computer-science student at BITS Pilani who couldn’t stop making things — running the visual-effects department, teaching myself software, and eventually designing and founding a psychedelic apparel brand that sold over 2,000 units at cultural festivals.

Since then I’ve worked as a visual & motion designer at an ed-tech company, a research facilitator studying minority student experience in art education, and now as an Event Lead & Ambassador for City of Maths — designing and delivering workshops that make mathematics feel human. I’m finishing an MA in Graphic Design at Leeds Arts University.

My research and practice are held together by one belief: creativity should be accessible. Not everyone has expensive software or a studio — but everyone has paper, a photocopier, and curiosity. Most of my favourite work is built from exactly that.
