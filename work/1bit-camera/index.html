1 Bit Camera

A palm-sized ESP32 camera that sees only in black and white, dithers each frame through a bank of 1-bit effects, and prints it as a postcard on a 57 mm thermal printer.

Role
Design & Engineering
Year
2026
Focus
Embedded · 1-bit imaging · Thermal print
Output
57 mm thermal postcards

A camera that refuses colour. An ESP32-S3 board reads an OV5640 sensor, crushes every frame down to pure black and white, and runs it through a bank of 1-bit effects — Floyd–Steinberg, Bayer, halftone, pixel and more — before sending it over Bluetooth to a 57 mm thermal printer. I designed the hardware and enclosure, wrote the firmware, tuned the dithering pipeline and built a browser simulator so the whole thing can be played with before the printer ever warms up.
