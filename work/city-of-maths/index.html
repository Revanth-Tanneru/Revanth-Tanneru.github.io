As Event Lead & Ambassador at City of Maths, I plan, design and deliver interactive educational workshops in schools — experiences that combine play with genuine mathematical content, and make research ideas accessible to everyone.

[ 2 images · click any image to enlarge ]

01

Venn cricket bowling machine — with the University of Cambridge. Hundreds of schoolchildren in Leeds built working, small-scale replicas of the historic Venn cricket bowling machine as part of the UK’s first City of Maths programme. Designed for pupils to build themselves, the machines teach that maths is about curiosity, creativity and solving real-world problems — not just numbers and equations. Pupils tested launch angles, measured distance travelled, and met the full-size replica in Leeds city centre.

02

The project was covered by the University of Cambridge’s Department of Engineering, which named me (as Revanth Tanneeru) among the City of Maths Ambassadors alongside Thomas Glenday, Head of Design and Technical Services.
