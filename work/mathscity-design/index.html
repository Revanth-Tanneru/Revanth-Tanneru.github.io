Mathscity Flyer & Zine

A flyer people wouldn’t throw away — its back folds into a decahedron, tiled by a non-periodic tessellation of organic (dinosaur) shapes.

Role
Design Lead
Year
2025 — Present
Focus
Print · Flyers · Zine · Tessellation · Interactive · Generative
Pages
4 spreads · 6 images

I wanted a flyer that doesn’t get thrown in the bin. So the back isn’t a footer — it’s a fold: cut along the net, fold the tabs, and the sheet becomes a decahedron you keep on your desk. Every face is covered by a non-periodic, pentagonal tessellation of organic tiles — dinosaurs, because why not. The tile is generated by code so it tiles the pentagon completely: edge to edge, no gaps. Try it below — drag the tile and the printable net redraws itself.
