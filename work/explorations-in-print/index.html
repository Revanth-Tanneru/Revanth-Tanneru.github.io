Print, Xerox & Collage · 2025

Explorations in Print

Five pieces made entirely without digital tools — photocopier distortion, cut-up text, found media.

Role
Designer & Researcher
Year
2025
Focus
Photocopy · Collage · Protest Art · Poetry
Pages
23 pages

One part of a wider practice that spans screen and print alike. This module is a series of visual art pieces made entirely physically — no digital tools — exploring print through collage and photocopier technique. Five pieces probe the constraints, materiality, cost, composition and accessibility of the medium: the NHS Protest Poster, Becoming, Defiance, Time and Puzzle. The full module runs page by page.
