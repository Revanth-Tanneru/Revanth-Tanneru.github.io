A 1.5-hour creative workshop that teaches how to get inspired by other art forms — music, writing, anything — and turn that influence into visual art. The aim is to help people trust their instincts and understand the context behind aesthetic choices. The whole session deck — structure, audience, demo, feedback — runs here as it was designed.
