Film, Storyboard & Direction · 2022

48 Hour Film Challenge

“Dance Baby Dance”: a short film about overcoming the fear of dancing in public, which we wrote, shot and edited in 48 hours for the 12th season of the India Film Project (IFP).

Role
Co-director & Designer
Year
2022
Focus
Film · Storyboards · Direction · 48 Hour Challenge · Design
Pages
4 pages

With the theme of “overcoming fear”, we wrote and shot a short film about a young man facing his fear of dancing in public. From brainstorming to filming, it was all hands on deck. The storyboard served as our roadmap, guiding each scene’s choreography and camera angles. We made it in 48 hours and it still earned a nomination.
